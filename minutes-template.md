# Meeting Minutes Template

> **Note:** This is a guide/template which you do not need to use. Feel free
> to adapt it to your team's needs.

## Meeting Heading Format

Use the following format for meeting headings:
- Format: `Iter{number}-{week}-{day}`
- Example: `Iter1-06-Monday` or `Iter0-02-Wednesday`
